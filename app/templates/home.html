Why LoopClose

{# ── How it works ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────── #}
How it works

From conversation to closed loop in four steps

  1. 1

    Capture the meeting

    Schedule a meeting or handover, add participants and an agenda, then paste or type the raw notes.

  2. 2

    Get an AI recap

    AI drafts a summary, key decisions and proposed action items. You review and edit before anything is saved.

  3. 3

    Assign & track

    Each item gets an owner, priority and due date. Overdue and unowned work is flagged on the dashboard.

  4. 4

    Carry forward & close

    Open items roll into the next meeting until they are closed with a recorded outcome.
